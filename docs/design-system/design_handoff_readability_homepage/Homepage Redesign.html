Design Language Review · Readability Pass

The luxury holds. The legibility didn't.

Three things were quietly costing readability: a secondary text tone too pale to pass, gold used as text on cream, and a type scale that bottomed out at 9–10px. Below is the recommendation — same warm, editorial character, now legible. Everything beneath it is the live homepage with the fix applied.

01 — Text colour

Darken the secondary tone

Body & meta text used #A89880 on cream — about 2.5:1, failing AA. A deeper warm taupe keeps the parchment mood and clears 6:1.

Was Organize what you own 2.5:1 · fails
→
Now Organize what you own 6.1:1 · AA
02 — The gold

Split it in two

Bright gold #C9A84C is only ~2:1 on cream. Keep it for dark panels & accents; introduce an antique gold for prices & labels on light.

Was · on cream $16,900 2.1:1 · fails
→
Now · antique $16,900 4.8:1 · AA
Keep · on dark $16,900 7.7:1 · AAA
03 — Type scale

Lift the floor

Body went 13→15px, captions 12→14px, and the 9–10px labels to a hard 11–12px floor. Display sizes grew for more editorial confidence.

Body13px→ 15px
Caption12px→ 14px
UI label10px→ 12PX
Min9px→ 11px floor
